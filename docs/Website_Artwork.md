of Winston, the Great Dane who inspired Animal Tracker. Generated with the

## Generation prompt

Create a polished editorial cartoon for Animal Tracker, landscape 3:2.
Use the two supplied dog photographs as subject references only. Depict Winston,
a large dark charcoal Great Dane with a broad heavy muzzle, drooping jowls,
natural floppy ears, long powerful legs, and a pale blue/cream patterned collar.
Show him reclining on a muted sage cushion, long front legs extending beyond
it, head raised in a friendly three-quarter view and a small pink tongue showing.
Place a small brown plush bear near his paws, inspired by his real stuffed toys.
Use clean storybook cartoon outlines, restrained gouache shading, subtle paper
grain, and a warm ivory background. Match the site's forest-green, sage, cream,
charcoal and terracotta palette. Keep the whole dog visible. No people, property
details, camera screenshots, lettering, logos, or watermarks.
